# Sketch-to-map
Turn a pencil sketch into a cute, print-ready venue map with AI.

Sketch-to-Map Prompt

Turn a hand-drawn floor plan into a clean, cute, print-ready venue map, with an AI assistant like Claude.

Made for hackathons, workshops, offices, pop-ups, weddings: any space where people need to find their spot fast.

**What you need**

A photo of your sketch. Pencil on paper is fine. Draw rooms, tables, doors and anything people should find (kitchen, screen, printer...).
Your colors. Pick one way:

Take a picture of something with your brand colors (a poster, a slide, your logo), or

Type the color codes (for example 
#16143A, 
#F2EE5B, 
#5CCB8C, 
#3E3BEA).

Your list (optional): team or guest names, and how many people are in each.

Your print size (optional): A3, A4, poster...

**The prompt:**

Can you turn my hand-drawn sketch into a clean, cute floor map?

THE SKETCH
I attached a photo of my drawing. Keep everything where I drew it.
Numbers on tables = how many people sit there.
If something in the sketch isn't clear (a shape with no label, a line that
could be a wall or a door), ask me before guessing.

THE COLORS
Choose one (delete the other):
- Take the colors from the picture I attached: [e.g. "our event poster"]
- Use these color codes: [#______, #______, #______, #______]
Use these different colors across the map, keeping the exact same colors.

THE LOOK
- Nice and cute: rounded corners, soft shapes, simple icons, no clutter.
- Each room gets its own outlined box, with a small gap between rooms.
- Label the rooms: [e.g. Event Room, Meeting Room, Kitchen, Storage].
- Add a big "YOU ARE HERE" dot next to the entrance.
- Add a small key at the bottom that explains the colors.

THE TABLES
- Instead of numbers, show who sits where, like: Team Name (4).
- Color each table by group size, using my picked colors (one color per size).
- Use the same font size for every table name, and make sure every name fits.
- Here's my list: [Table A: Name (4), Table B: Name (3)...]
  (If you're not sure which table is which, put small letters on the
  tables first and I'll tell you who goes where.)

EXTRAS (keep or delete)
- Toilet sign: [e.g. Women: 1st floor / Men: ground floor]
- Event name or logo at the top: [your text, or attach the logo]
- [anything else: printer, coat rack, charging spot...]

PRINT
Make it ready to print on [A3] portrait, at actual size, with the
background color filling the page.

Make it as an editable design, so I can move things myself.
